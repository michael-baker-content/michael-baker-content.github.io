One of my current portfolio projects is Baker Parks. My aim is to interact with public data and provide additional context. I'm trying to give a different spin on how this sort of information is presented.

I built the foundation of this site last month, and since then I've been traveling to park sites to take photos I can use in Baker Parks to bring color and a better sense of place. I'm also using secondhand photos available online with Creative Commons licenses. 

I'm currently surveying parks in the San Francisco area only, but I could expand to include other Bay area places in the future.

[San Francisco's parks website](https://sfrecpark.org) presents a lot of the same information you'll find on my site. Their key park data is public via their extremely useful API.
